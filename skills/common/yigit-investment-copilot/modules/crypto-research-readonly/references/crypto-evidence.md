# Crypto evidence

Prefer:

1. Official chain explorer, protocol documentation, verified contract and governance repository.
2. Official exchange market-data endpoints for quotes and candles.
3. Independent aggregators for cross-checking identity, venues and last-updated time.
4. Reputable on-chain analytics with transparent methodology.
5. Social or community content only as unverified sentiment.

Capture network, contract, pair, venue, quote currency, source, effective timestamp, fetched timestamp, candle interval, latency, and whether a metric is raw, estimated or self-reported.

Useful read-only sources include official Binance, Coinbase and Kraken market-data APIs plus CoinGecko or CoinMarketCap for corroboration. Coverage and terms must be checked at use time.
