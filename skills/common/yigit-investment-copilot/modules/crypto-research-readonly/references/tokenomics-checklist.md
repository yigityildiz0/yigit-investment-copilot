# Tokenomics checklist

- Utility and who must buy or hold the token
- Circulating, total and maximum supply definitions
- Emission, burn, staking reward and inflation schedule
- Team, investor, foundation and community allocation
- Vesting cliffs and dated unlocks
- Treasury assets, liabilities, runway and token dependence
- Governance power, delegation and concentration
- Value accrual from protocol usage, fees or buybacks
- Validator/miner economics and security budget
- Cross-chain representations and bridge dependencies

Calculate fully diluted value only with a clear supply definition. Do not confuse protocol revenue with token-holder cash flow.
