# Crypto risk checklist

- Contract exploits, upgrade keys and privileged roles
- Oracle, bridge, sequencer and validator failure
- Stablecoin or collateral depeg
- Exchange/custodian concentration and withdrawal risk
- Thin liquidity, spoofing, wash trading and venue dispersion
- Leverage, funding, liquidation cascades and basis dislocation
- Insider unlocks, treasury sales and governance capture
- Regulatory, sanctions, listing and delisting risk
- Chain congestion, outages and reorgs
- Narrative dependence without measurable usage

State custody and venue risks separately from the asset thesis.
