# Market regime protocol

## Axis ledger

Record current value, direction, source, effective period, fetch time, revision status and horizon for:

- Price trend and breadth
- Realized and implied volatility
- Volume, depth, spread and funding liquidity
- Policy rate, real rate and yield curve
- Inflation, growth and labor direction
- FX and commodity transmission
- Credit spreads/default conditions
- Earnings level and revisions
- Positioning/crowding when defensible
- Dated policy, earnings and geopolitical events

Do not force a label until the axes are visible. Mixed signals are a valid result.

## Historical comparison

Use point-in-time data where revisions matter. Define how episodes were selected, report count and dispersion, and identify structural differences. Historical analogues support scenario priors; they do not prove the next path.

## Transition table

For each plausible next regime state, provide:

- Observable confirming measurements
- Assets/factors likely helped and hurt
- Expected horizon
- Failure or reversal signal
- Confidence and evidence gap

Keep this separate from a security's valuation, catalyst and execution decision.
