# Interpretation framework

## Structure before indicators

1. Higher highs/lows or lower highs/lows
2. Range boundaries and failed breaks
3. Gaps, event levels and anchored volume areas when supported
4. Liquidity and spread
5. Volatility regime

## Indicator use

- Moving averages: trend and distance, not automatic support.
- RSI: momentum regime and divergence with confirmation, not a standalone reversal.
- MACD: trend/momentum change; lag increases around fast events.
- ATR: volatility-scaled invalidation and position risk, not direction.
- Volume: participation and breakout quality; compare like sessions and adjusted data.

Require at least one price-structure reason and one independent contextual reason before treating an indicator setup as decision-relevant.

## Scenario template

- Bull: confirmation trigger, target zone, time horizon, failure level.
- Base: expected range, chop/decay costs, reassessment trigger.
- Bear: breakdown trigger, downside zone, gap/liquidity risk.

Targets are conditional paths, not promises.
