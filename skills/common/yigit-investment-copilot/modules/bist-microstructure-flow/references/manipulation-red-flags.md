# Manipulation and pump-and-dump red flags (BIST)

These are risk flags, not accusations. Two or more → position size tiny or zero, no chasing, pre-defined exit.

- **Small free float** (<15–20%) and small market value, with a sudden multi-fold rise in volume.
- **Limit-up streaks** (3+ tavan in 20 sessions) or a >100% rise in 3 months without a matching KAP fundamental event.
- **Pump-and-crash footprint**: ≥2.5× run-up from a 6-month base, then below half of the peak (`POMPA_COKUS` flag in the scan).
- **Social/Telegram hype**, "hedef 3 kat", paid signal groups, influencer campaigns; stories about bedelsiz, big contracts or foreign buyers before any KAP disclosure.
- **Rumor-response disclosures** ("sosyal medyada çıkan haberler hakkında") and denials.
- **Supply signals after a spike**: `Borsada İşlem Gören Tipe Dönüşüm`, pay satış bilgi formu, insider selling (`Pay Alım Satım Bildirimi` read for direction), large holders' block sales.
- **Regulatory signals**: VBTS measures (açığa satış/kredi yasağı → brüt takas → tek fiyat), SPK trading bans naming the stock, frequent stock-level circuit breakers.
- **Price/fundamental disconnect**: market value many times book with losses or tiny revenue, P/E in the hundreds without growth evidence.

What to do:

1. Say which flags fired and what they usually precede (sharp reversals, illiquidity, being unable to exit).
2. If the user still wants to trade it, treat it as lottery-like speculative capital: small fixed amount, no averaging down, exit plan written in advance, never with borrowed money.
3. Prefer liquid names with fundamental catalysts for the same horizon.
