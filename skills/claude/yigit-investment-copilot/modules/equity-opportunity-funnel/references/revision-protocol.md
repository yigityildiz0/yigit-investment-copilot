# Recommendation revision protocol

Every final recommendation must have a version record:

- Recommendation ID and issue time
- Instrument, action and horizon
- Price/data cutoff
- Thesis and decisive evidence
- Bear/base/bull ranges
- Entry, invalidation and review date/event
- Confidence and unresolved items

On a follow-up:

1. Restate the objective without naming the incumbent and, when practical, perform a fresh screen before reviewing the old rationale. The incumbent receives no consistency bonus.
2. Retrieve or reconstruct the prior version from the active conversation or user-provided record.
3. Compare price, elapsed time, filings, news, catalyst status, forecasts and thesis-kill conditions on a common data cutoff.
4. Challenge the incumbent against the best same-asset runner-up, relevant cross-asset alternatives and `do nothing`. Exclude non-comparable asset classes with a reason; do not force novelty.
5. Classify the result:
   - `UNCHANGED`: no material evidence changed.
   - `UPDATED`: new evidence or price/time movement changed risk/reward.
   - `INVALIDATED`: a stated thesis-kill condition occurred.
   - `CORRECTION`: the earlier analysis was shallow, wrong or missed material evidence.
6. Show the exact delta before the new action and apply full recommendation-grade diligence to any replacement.

Do not reverse `AL` to `SAT` on unchanged evidence without declaring a correction and explaining the original defect. Do not defend a bad old call merely for consistency; honest correction is preferable to silent contradiction.
