# News source map

Rank sources by who owns the fact. Lower tiers find stories; higher tiers confirm them.

| Tier | Türkiye | Global |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Primary | KAP disclosures and financial reports; SPK bülteni; Borsa İstanbul duyuruları/VBTS; TCMB (PPK, rezervler, EVDS); TÜİK; Resmî Gazete; Hazine ve Maliye Bakanlığı; company IR | Regulators (SEC EDGAR), central banks (Fed, ECB), statistics offices, exchanges, company filings |
| 2 Licensed terminals | Matriks, Foreks, Rasyonet, Bloomberg HT terminal feeds | Bloomberg, Refinitiv/LSEG, FactSet |
| 3 Reputable press | AA Finans, Bloomberg HT, Dünya, Ekonomim, Reuters Türkçe, Habertürk Ekonomi (check the named source inside the story) | Reuters, Bloomberg News, FT, WSJ, Nikkei |
| 4 Aggregators / analysis | İş Yatırım, Fintables, broker research notes (conflicts of interest), investing.com | Seeking Alpha, aggregator sites |
| 5 Social | X, Telegram, YouTube, forum posts | Same |

Rules:

- Confirm tier 3–5 stories in tier 1 before they change a decision.
- Broker research and target prices are opinions with incentives; use them as "what consensus may expect", never as proof.
- Syndicated copies are one source. Count independent confirmations, not headlines.
- For Turkish macro, the release calendar matters: TÜİK CPI (usually the 3rd business day of the month, 10:00), TCMB PPK (announced calendar, 14:00), TCMB weekly reserves (Thursday), SPK bulletin (weekly). Verify dates on the official calendars each time.
