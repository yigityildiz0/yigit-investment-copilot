# Türkiye macro regime

Use point-in-time official data and publication vintages where possible.

## Regime map

- Inflation direction and composition
- Policy rate, real rate, yield curve and liquidity
- TRY trend, reserves and FX pass-through
- Credit growth, deposit mix and financial conditions
- Domestic demand, production, labor and external balance
- Fiscal impulse, administered prices and regulation
- Global rates, dollar, commodities and risk appetite

## Transmission

For each material macro variable, state:

1. Current evidence and release date
2. Direction and uncertainty
3. Revenue effect
4. Margin/cash-flow effect
5. Balance-sheet and financing effect
6. Valuation multiple or discount-rate effect
7. Winners, losers, lag, and conditions that reverse the effect

Do not use revised data in a historical backtest as if it had been known at the time.
