# BIST equity workflow

1. Confirm ticker, company legal name, share class, market segment, and currency.
2. Read the latest KAP financials, audit/review opinion, notes, special disclosures, investor presentation, and corporate actions.
3. Determine consolidated versus standalone basis and whether TMS 29 inflation accounting applies. Separate monetary gain/loss and real operating change from nominal growth.
4. Normalize revenue, EBITDA/operating profit, net income, operating cash flow, net debt, working capital, share count, dilution, related-party items, one-offs, and sector KPIs.
5. Compare at least three suitable peers using consistent periods and definitions. Explain why each peer belongs.
6. Use DCF, multiples, asset value, or sum-of-parts only when suitable for the business. Show assumptions and sensitivity.
7. Check free float, average value traded, bid-ask spread, ownership concentration, pledges, unusual corporate actions, and governance.
8. Map rates, inflation, FX, commodity, regulation, and demand to revenue, margins, cash flow, and valuation.
9. State the expectation already priced in, the variant view, catalysts, and prove/kill conditions.

For a 1–4 week request, do not skip fundamentals; compress them into balance-sheet/event risk and use technical timing as a separate layer.
