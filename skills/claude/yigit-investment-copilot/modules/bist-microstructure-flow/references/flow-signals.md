# Flow signals and how to read them

| Signal | What it is | Useful reading | Common mistake |
|---|---|---|---|
| Relative volume | Today's volume ÷ 10–60 day average | >1.5–2× with a range breakout supports the move; high volume on a down day near highs can mark distribution | Comparing a half-day with a full day; ignoring index-wide volume |
| Value traded (TL) | Price × volume | Sets feasible position size (keep orders ≤1–2% of median daily value) | Using share volume across very different prices |
| Spread and depth | Best bid/ask gap and quantity near the touch | Multi-tick spreads and thin depth = high cost and gap risk | Treating displayed depth as guaranteed liquidity (orders can be pulled) |
| VWAP | Volume-weighted average price of the session | Price holding above VWAP on rising volume = buyers in control intraday | Using VWAP for multi-week decisions |
| AKD (aracı kurum dağılımı) | Net buying/selling by brokerage houses | Persistent net buying by several institutions over days, with price stable/up, is supportive | One-day AKD; assuming a broker's clients act together; ignoring retail brokers' aggregate nature |
| Takas (settlement/custody distribution) | Holdings by custodian changes over T+2 | Rising custody at institutional custodians over weeks can indicate accumulation | Reading takas as ownership of specific people |
| Limit-up/limit-down queues | Unfilled orders at ±10% | Large queues and shrinking sell side can continue a streak; queue collapse often starts reversal | Chasing a limit-up queue with a market order |
| Circuit breaker | Stock-level halt/auction after extreme moves (KAP notice) | Frequent triggers = fragile, news- or crowd-driven trading | Treating it as a signal of direction |
| Foreign ownership share | Share of free float held by foreign investors (Borsa İstanbul/MKK statistics) | Sustained rises in liquid names can accompany re-rating | Short-term noise; custody artefacts |
| Short interest / açığa satış | Short-sale volume where permitted | Crowded shorts plus good news can squeeze | Short sales are restricted in many names and under VBTS measures |

Persistence rule: a flow signal counts only if it repeats over several sessions and agrees with price/volume; otherwise record it as noise.
