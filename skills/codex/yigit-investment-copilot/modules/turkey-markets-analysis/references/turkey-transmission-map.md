## Checklist for a stock

1. Revenue currency mix (export share from statements) and cost currency mix.
2. Net FX position (statements item "Net Yabancı Para Pozisyonu") and hedging.
3. Debt: TL vs FX, fixed vs floating, maturities.
4. Pricing power under inflation (can margins be defended?).
5. Regulated prices or caps (energy, telecom, food).
6. Sensitivity of valuation multiple to real rates (long-duration growth vs cash cows).
