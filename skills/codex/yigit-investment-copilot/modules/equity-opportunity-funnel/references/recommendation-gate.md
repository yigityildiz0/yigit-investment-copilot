# Recommendation-grade gate

Do not issue `AL`, `SPEKÜLATİF AL`, or `ARTIR` until every applicable item is either cleared or explicitly conditional with a defined trigger.

## Identity and evidence

- Exact security/share class, venue and currency
- Timestamped current or executable price with delay status
- Latest primary filing plus material subsequent disclosures
- Critical numbers reconciled by Finance Evidence Guard

## Thesis and fundamentals

- Falsifiable thesis and variant perception
- Relevant operating/sector KPIs
- Earnings quality, cash flow, balance sheet, dilution and financing risk
- What is already priced in and why the market may be wrong

## Valuation and forecast

- Appropriate valuation method or a documented reason valuation is not decision-critical for the horizon
- Bear/base/bull values and transparent assumptions
- Probabilistic price/return range matched to the horizon
- Downside mechanism and severe-loss probability

## Timing and execution

- Technical regime, entry gate and invalidation
- Dated catalyst path or a clear non-event thesis
- Liquidity, bid/ask, fees, lot size and realistic exit
- Quantity inputs and portfolio concentration

## Challenge and comparison

- Red-team verdict is PASS or a precisely bounded CONDITIONAL
- Best runner-up and do-nothing/benchmark were compared
- No unresolved critical source conflict
- Monitoring and exit/review rule are explicit

A failed gate sends the name back to `FİNALİST`, `ADAY`, or `ELENDİ`; it does not become a weaker recommendation.
