# Exit playbook (apply in order; the first rule that fires wins)

| # | Question | If yes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Did a non-price kill condition occur (thesis break: guidance cut, dilution, governance/SPK issue, lost contract, sector shock)? | **SAT** now, regardless of price. |
| 2 | Did price close below the written stop (or open below it)? | **SAT** at the first reasonable opportunity; do not wait for a rebound. |
| 3 | Did the market regime turn to "DÜŞÜŞ TRENDİ" or distribution days cluster (≥5 in 25 sessions) while the stock is weak vs XU100? | **AZALT** to the regime band; tighten stops on the rest. |
| 4 | Time stop: no +0.5R progress within ~⅓ of the horizon? | **AZALT/SAT** and free capital; the thesis may be right but slow. |
| 5 | Target reached or valuation above the bull case? | Take the planned partial; trail the rest; **SAT** if the upside to the new base case is below the risk. |
| 6 | Extended: price > 25% above SMA50, RSI > 80, parabolic/limit-up streak, heavy volume reversal day? | Take partial profits or tighten the trailing stop; do not add. |
| 7 | A clearly better opportunity exists (same horizon, higher expected value after switching costs, verified by the funnel)? | **SAT/AZALT** and switch; document the comparison. |
| 8 | Position above concentration limits after a rally, or correlation with other holdings too high? | **AZALT** to the limit (rebalance). |
| 9 | None of the above. | **TUT** and set the next review date/trigger. |

## Winners vs losers (disposition effect guard)

People tend to sell winners early and keep losers hoping to break even (Shefrin & Statman; Odean). Counter-rules:

- The purchase price is irrelevant to the decision; ask "would I buy it today at this price with this evidence?".
- Losers: the stop and the kill condition decide, not the hope of getting back to cost.
- Winners: let the trailing stop decide unless rules 1, 5, 6 or 8 fire.

## Adding to a position

Add only if: the position is above entry by ≥1R, the thesis is intact or strengthened, the regime allows, the new total risk (with the stop on the whole position) stays within the loss budget, and concentration limits hold.

## After the exit

Journal: entry/exit dates and prices, R result, which rule fired, what the plan said, deviations, lesson (process vs outcome). Resolve the forecast in the ledger when the horizon ends.
