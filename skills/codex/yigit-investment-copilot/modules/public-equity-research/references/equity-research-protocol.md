# Public equity research protocol

## Minimum primary packet

- Latest annual and interim filing with notes
- Latest earnings release and call/transcript only as management commentary
- Current share count, diluted instruments and capital changes
- Debt, cash, maturity and covenant disclosures
- Segment and geographic reporting
- Material regulatory/exchange filings and dated corporate actions
- Current security price, currency, venue, timestamp and adjustment status

Record source, period, publication/effective date, accounting/consolidation basis and units for each decisive claim.

## Normalization ledger

Bridge reported to decision-useful figures visibly. Review acquisitions/disposals, discontinued operations, restructuring, impairment, fair-value changes, stock compensation, leases, capitalized development, working capital, related parties, tax, inflation accounting and currency translation. Do not remove a recurring “one-off”.

## Valuation discipline

Use methods that fit the business and state why. Show current price-implied assumptions, scenario ranges and sensitivities. Reconcile enterprise/equity value, net debt, minorities, associates, pensions, options and diluted share count. Compare peers only after normalizing growth, margins, accounting, geography, cyclicality and leverage.

## Thesis gate

A recommendation-grade thesis needs:

- What appears priced in
- Evidence-backed differentiated view
- Falsifiable operating/financial pillars and KPIs
- Dated catalyst or recognition path
- Strongest contradictory evidence
- Bear/base/bull valuation and return ranges
- Kill/add/trim/exit conditions
- Relevant peer/alternative and no-action comparison
- Liquidity, portfolio fit and execution limits

If the evidence, identity or valuation basis is incomplete, return `RESEARCH INCOMPLETE` or `WATCH`, not a disguised buy call.
